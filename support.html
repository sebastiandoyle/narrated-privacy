Help

The narrator refused my recap. Everything is checked by an automatic safety filter. Try describing your day without anything graphic, hateful or explicit.

I hit a daily limit. Narrations have a fair-use daily limit that resets each day. Narrated Pro raises it.

A narration was offensive or wrong. Open it and tap the flag at the top right. We review every report and the narration is removed from your library.

Restore or cancel a subscription. Restore in Settings inside the app. Cancel in your App Store account settings on your iPhone.
